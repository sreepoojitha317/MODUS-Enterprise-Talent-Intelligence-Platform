How to Interpret Your Scores

CCI — Current Capability Index

Reflects what you are able to demonstrate today through your assessment responses and practical evidence.

CPI — Candidate Potential Index

Represents your development potential, including indicators such as learning ability, adaptability, motivation and consistency.

CRI — Client Readiness Index

Indicates how prepared you are to apply your capability in practical consulting and client-oriented situations.

Evidence Confidence

Indicates how strongly the assessment result is supported by the quality, completeness and consistency of the available evidence.
